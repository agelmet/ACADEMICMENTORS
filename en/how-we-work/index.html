The process, step by step

How we work

From the first message to your article’s acceptance, you always know where the work stands and who is doing it.

1. Request and free quote

Fill in the form with your field, service, length and deadline. You can attach your files to the e-mail you receive. Within a few hours we send a quote and timeline, with no obligation.

2. A mentor from your field

A PhD holder experienced in your field takes over. Work starts with a deposit and the rest is split into 2 or 3 interest-free instalments tied to progress.

3. Staged delivery

Each section comes to you for review. You comment, ask for changes and follow progress from your account. Nothing moves to the next stage without your approval.

4. Final delivery and submission

You receive the final text with a Turnitin plagiarism and AI report. We help with the cover letter, submission and responses to reviewers.

5. Revisions for 30 days

For 30 days after final delivery we make any changes you need at no charge, as long as they do not change the original specifications.
